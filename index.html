Policy outlook & key risks
September is now the decision point. The July "hawkish hold" with three dissents leaves the committee one hot inflation print away from its first hike since 2023 — futures price ~75% odds of a September move. The wild card is oil: a durable US–Iran pause could pull headline inflation back and unwind the hike trade as fast as it built. Warsh's five task forces report this fall, so a September hike would mean acting before his own reviews conclude.
Hawkish case
Three voters already dissented for an immediate hike; Waller sits on the edge. CPI 4.2% YoY, core PCE 3.4%. Oil topped $100 in mid-July and July crude is still up ~20% on the month, keeping headline inflation hot. The 30Y yield at 5.19% (highest since 2007) shows the market pricing higher-for-longer.
Dovish case
A US–Iran pause already knocked Brent from $100+ toward $88, collapsing July hike odds from 38% to single digits within days — energy relief could repeat. June payrolls were weak (+57k). Warsh believes AI is ultimately disinflationary and may prefer to wait for his task forces. Trump keeps up public pressure for lower rates, calling the board "political."
